# Polar Drop — Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback: email **jack@perkins.net** with "Polar Drop" in the subject line.

Common questions:

- **Lost progress?** Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
- **Ads?** Ads are optional and only offered for a revive, a puzzle hint or double XP. You can always decline.
- **Privacy?** See the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
