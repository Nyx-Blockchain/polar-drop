# Polar Drop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Operator: Jack Perkins (United Kingdom). Contact: jack@perkins.net.

## Summary
Polar Drop works without an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. The app shows optional rewarded ads and, only if you agree, collects anonymous gameplay analytics so we can tune difficulty and keep the game free.

## What we collect and why
**Nothing until you choose.** On first launch we ask your year of birth so we can apply the right protections (no personalised ads and no tracking prompt for players under 16 in the UK/EEA or under 13 elsewhere). The year is stored only on your device.

**Gameplay analytics (with your consent).** If you allow analytics, we receive anonymous events about how you play: when a session or run starts and ends, how long it lasted, your score and grade, how each drop went (tiles cleared, chains, magnet shots, board density), puzzle results, missions and levels, which ad offers you saw or accepted, and whether you shared a result. The only context attached is your device class (for example "iPhone 12 class"), iOS version (major.minor) and the country from your device's region setting. We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, anything you type, or any identifier that names you. These events are processed by Google Firebase Analytics and may be exported to Google BigQuery for analysis.

**Ads.** Polar Drop shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads are only ever offered for a revive, a puzzle hint or a double-XP bonus; you can always decline. In the UK/EEA we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. After your first completed run, iOS may ask whether to allow tracking; if you allow it, the ad network may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you still get ads, just not personalised ones. Players under the age thresholds above never see the tracking prompt and only receive non-personalised ads. Ad networks may collect the data listed in their own policies ([Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543), [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)), including IP address (approximate location), device information and ad interaction data.

**Difficulty tuning.** In Endless mode the game may adjust its pacing based on your recent runs (on your device) or on a remotely configured profile. Ranked modes (Daily, Hardcore, puzzles and the daily puzzle) are identical for everyone and never adapt.

**Game Center.** If you are signed in to Game Center, scores for Daily, Hardcore and the daily puzzle are submitted to Apple's leaderboards under Apple's privacy policy.

**Sharing.** Score cards, emoji results and clips are created on your device and only leave it when you choose to share them.

## Your choices
- Decline or withdraw analytics and ad consent at any time from the app's Privacy options (UK/EEA) or your device settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).
- Delete the app to remove all local data (progress, settings, birth year). Analytics data already collected is anonymous and retained by Firebase for 2 months (the default user-level retention) before aggregation; see [Firebase privacy and security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Children
Polar Drop is suitable for all ages. We apply stricter defaults (no personalised ads, no tracking prompt, no analytics) to players under 16 in the UK/EEA and under 13 elsewhere, based on the birth year you enter.
